How it works

What you send

  • Screens8–12 exported images or Figma frames. They become the states of the story.
  • Accent colourOne colour. Everything else stays black and white.
  • FontsThe names, or the font files.
  • LogoAn SVG if you have one.
  • Last line of copyThe line that ends the video and opens the loop.

What you get

  • loop.mp41440 × 1440 at 60 fps, or 1920 × 1080 if you ask.
  • scene.htmlThe same animation as a page. Drag the timeline to any frame.
  • click-track.wavA 120 BPM click track. Every action lands on a beat.

The rules Siya works by

  • One continuous morph. No cuts and no fades between states.
  • A real cursor drives every change: hover, click, drag and type.
  • The camera zooms until each state fills the frame, then pulls back.
  • Springs with a little mass and a tiny overshoot. No bouncy easing.
  • One action per beat at 120 BPM. The opening holds for 2 beats and nothing stands still for 4.
  • No particles, glows or gradients on the interface.
